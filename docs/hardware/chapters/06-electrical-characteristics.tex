\chapter{Electrical Characteristics}
\label{ch:electrical-characteristics}

The supply is 5~V $\pm$ 5\%: VCC1 and VCC2 are connected to it and GND1 and GND2 to the 0~V rail (see
Chapter~\ref{ch:signal-descriptions}). The figures in this chapter are provisional. They are targets the device is
designed to meet, and will be confirmed or revised when the device is characterised. All of them apply over the supply
range above and an ambient temperature of 0 to 70~\textdegree{}C.

\section{AC Characteristics}

Every time is measured from or to the 1.4~V point of the rising edge of \texttt{CLKI}, with the outputs loaded by
50~pF. The edge is the one that begins or ends a bus cycle (Section~\ref{sec:clock-edges}). Outputs change after the
edge that starts a cycle, and inputs are sampled at the edge that ends it.

\begin{table}[H]
	\centering
	\begin{tabular}{llrrl}
		\toprule
		\textbf{Parameter}                                                                                                                      & \textbf{Symbol} & \textbf{Min} & \textbf{Max} & \textbf{Unit} \\
		\midrule
		\texttt{CLKI} frequency                                                                                                                 & $f_{CLKI}$      & 9.0          & 12.0         & MHz           \\
		\texttt{CLKI} high time                                                                                                                 & $t_{CH}$        & 35           &              & ns            \\

		Output hold                                                                                                                             & $t_{OH}$        & 3            &              & ns            \\
		D0--D15 float, after the edge that ends a write                                                                                         & $t_{OZ}$        &              & 25           & ns            \\

\noindent The CPU itself uses $t_{OV} + t_{IS} = 35$~ns of every access. The address, the select and the memory share the
rest: 58~ns of a one-cycle access (93.1~ns at 10.74~MHz), and a further 93.1~ns for each wait state.
